Adaptive Teleoperation Motion Scaling Based on Human Performance Characterization
Surgical Robotics Teleoperation HRI
JHU SMARTS Lab
Human-subject study (n=8, 576 trials) on the da Vinci Research Kit characterizing how operators adapt hand velocity to motion scaling changes. Fitted a power-law model (R²>0.92) revealing operators compensate only 20–43% of scaling changes, with adaptation varying substantially across individuals. Showed tracking error is amplified in low-curvature, high-speed segments and identified a 0.7s deceleration window at direction changes that adaptive systems must respect.
Submitted to IROS 2026
